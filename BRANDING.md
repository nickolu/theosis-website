# THEOSIS — Branding Guide  
## Infernal Cathedral Theme

This branding direction expresses the dark, destructive, ritualistic identity of THEOSIS.  
It blends gothic architecture, hellfire, and blackened ritual elements into a cohesive visual system.

---

## 1. Color Palette

| Name | Hex | Description |
|------|------|-------------|
| **Endless Darkness** | `#000000` | Primary background. Total void. |
| **Cursed Crimson** | `#b6344e` | Blood-ritual red used for accents and highlights. |
| **Blistering Heat** | `#dd8d52` | Lava-orange highlight for UI elements, CTAs, and visual flame motifs. |
| **Primordial Ooze** | `#573861` (or `#373861` depending on tile) | Dark purple-black to introduce mystical depth and break monotone blacks. |

Use primarily black + crimson, with heat and ooze as accent colors.

---

## 2. Typography

### **Display / Hero (Logos, Hero Banners, Main Titles)**
- **Primary:** `UnifrakturCook` (Bold)
- **Alternatives:**  
  - `UnifrakturMaguntia`  
  - `Grenze Gotisch` (Bold–Black)  
  - `MedievalSharp`  

Use at very large sizes only. Avoid long lines of text.

### **Secondary Headings**
- **Primary:** `Grenze Gotisch` (Regular / Semibold)
- **Alternatives:**  
  - `Cormorant Garamond`  
  - `Cardo`  
  - `Playfair Display`  

Provides structure and hierarchy while keeping the gothic tone.

### **Body / UI Text**
- **Primary:** `Roboto Serif`
- **Alternatives:**  
  - `Crimson Pro`  
  - `Lora`  
  - `Source Serif Pro`  

Readable, modern serifs that contrast well against ornate headers.

---

## 3. Motifs & Imagery

Visual motifs should include:
- gothic arches  
- burning crosses  
- demonic silhouettes  
- skulls and skeletal anatomy  
- cracked stone, ash, and smokey textures  
- fire-rimmed typography  
- red and orange glow effects  
- darkness swallowing negative space  

Use backgrounds with subtle noise, grain, or stone textures.  
Keep compositions dramatic but not overly busy.

---

## 4. UI Styling Notes

- Buttons: **Blistering Heat** background with **Endless Darkness** text.  
- Hover states: Shift toward **Cursed Crimson**.  
- Section dividers: Thin lines in **Primordial Ooze** or textured gothic motifs.  
- Nav bar: Black with subtle underglow (crimson or orange).  
- Link styles: Bone-white or crimson with low-key glow on hover.

---

## 5. Tone & Atmosphere

The site should evoke:
- ritual  
- danger  
- hellfire  
- gothic grandeur  
- darkness  
- violence  
- mystery  

The experience should feel like entering a cathedral in hell:  
grand, terrifying, reverent, and overwhelming.

---

For visual cohesion, rely heavily on:
- black backgrounds  
- serif body text  
- high-contrast blackletter display fonts  
- ritual motifs  
- crimson and ember accents  

This allows the brand to stay dark, heavy, and unmistakably THEOSIS.
